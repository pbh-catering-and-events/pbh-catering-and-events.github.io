Your Table, Your Menu

Design your menu, choose how it's served

Because we cook as well as decorate, you can build the exact menu you want. Choose your menu, then your serving style, then your dishes.

1 Choose your menu

BuffetYour dishes are set out on a styled buffet table and guests help themselves. Includes 1 starter, 2 mains, 2 starches, 2 sides and 1 dessert.P180 per guest
Set menuThe same dishes, served at the table. Your menu is printed on a card at every seat with your names and who it is prepared for. If you want each guest's own name on their card, message us your guest list on WhatsApp.P190 per guest (food P180 + personalised menu cards P10), plus your serving style

You can add up to 3 extra dishes: starter P20, main P40, starch P10, side P12, dessert P20 per guest.

2 Choose your serving style

For the Set menu only. A buffet is self-serve, so it does not need a serving style.

Family-style

Guests are seated and large platters and bowls are placed on each table for guests to pass around.

Best for family events, relaxed lunches and birthdaysChoose this style
Plated (American)

Each meal is plated and decorated in the kitchen and served to every guest at the table. Neat, quick and a real "wow" moment.

Best for weddings, formal dinners and corporate eventsChoose this style
Luxury (one waiter per table)

Every table has its own waiter for the whole event, who serves each course, keeps drinks topped up and clears the table.

Best for weddings, VIP tables and milestone celebrationsChoose this style

Family-style service is included. Plated service is P35 per guest and luxury service (one waiter per table) is P60 per guest, because they need extra waiters.

          FAQ
          
      
          Frequently asked questions
          
    
          
    
          
      
          Where can I read your Booking Terms?
          They are in the booking form, just above the "Send my booking request" button. Please read them before you send your request. Go to the Booking Terms
          
      
          Is my booking confirmed when I send the form?
          Not yet. The form sends us a booking request. We check it and email you within 48 hours to let you know if it is approved. Only then do you pay your deposit to secure your date.
          
      
          How do I pay?
          By bank transfer (EFT) to our FNBB account. Our payment details are shown on the booking form, and you use your name and event date as the reference. Send your proof of payment on WhatsApp or by email to admin@pbhgroup.co.bw.
          
      
          How much is the deposit, and when do I pay?
          The deposit is 50% of your instant quote, paid after we approve your booking by email. The balance is paid in one payment 7 days before your event. We don't offer instalments.
          
      
          Do you do décor only, or catering too?
          Both. You can book décor only, catering only, or both together, with one team for your whole event.
          
      
          Are delivery and set-up included?
          Yes. Delivery and collection within Gaborone, set-up and pack-up are included in every package and with all catering. Delivery outside Gaborone is added in your final quote.
          
      
          How early should I book?
          At least 3 months ahead for weddings, and 4-6 weeks for other events. Year-end and festive dates fill up fast.
          
      
          What if my guest numbers change?
          Confirm your final numbers 14 days before the event. Numbers can go up after that if stock is available, but the price won't reduce for fewer guests.
          
      
          How much do food and drinks cost?
          Our buffet is P180 per guest. A set menu, served at the table with personalised menu cards, is P190 per guest plus your serving style. Every drink, extra dish and serving style has a price per guest, and your instant quote adds them up as you choose. See the full list on our Price list.
          
      
          Can you cater for dietary needs?
          Yes: vegetarian, halal, diabetic and allergies. Tell us on the booking form or at least 14 days before your event.
